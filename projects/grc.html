Cybersecurity GRC Portfolio

A practical portfolio showing how I identify cyber risk, evaluate security controls, structure an RMF effort, document remediation, and communicate priorities to leadership.

Frameworks: NIST CSF 2.0RMF: NIST SP 800-37 Rev. 2Controls: NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5Assessment: 800-53A concepts

Scenario

Fictional company, realistic work.

This portfolio uses a fictional organization called HarborPoint Services, a 250-person hybrid professional-services company with Windows endpoints, cloud productivity tools, a SaaS CRM, remote access, customer personally identifiable information, and a small IT/security function.

Portfolio integrity: The organization, evidence, findings, and remediation records on these pages are simulated. They are built to demonstrate process and analytical ability without representing classroom exercises as paid client work or disclosing private systems.

Method

How I approach GRC work

1

Understand the system

Define assets, business processes, information types, users, dependencies, boundaries, and responsible owners.

2

Identify and rate risk

Connect threats and vulnerabilities to business impact, then score and prioritize the risk consistently.

3

Map and assess controls

Determine which controls should reduce the risk, what implementation should look like, and what evidence supports effectiveness.

4

Document findings

State the gap, evidence, business effect, owner, remediation action, due date, and verification method.

5

Translate for leadership

Explain material risk in plain business language so leaders can fund, prioritize, accept, or escalate it.

6

Monitor over time

Use measurable indicators, access reviews, vulnerability aging, test results, incidents, and change triggers to keep risk current.
