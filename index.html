About

Operational experience meets cybersecurity.

I am pursuing a B.S. in Cybersecurity Management & Policy at the University of Maryland Global Campus while building practical skills in risk management, security controls, networking, policy, and technical documentation.

I served in the U.S. Army from 1997 to 2014 in combat engineering, heavy equipment operations, controlled-area procedures, patrol and access-control responsibilities, and F-22 flight-line security support. That experience shaped how I approach accountability, risk, procedures, documentation, and mission-critical work.

My portfolio case studies use fictional organizations and simulated evidence so the work can be reviewed publicly without pretending that classroom or practice work came from a real client.
