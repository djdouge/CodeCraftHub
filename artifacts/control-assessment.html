NIST SP 800-53 Control Assessment

A simulated assessment sample for fictional HarborPoint Services. The purpose is to show how I connect a control requirement to implementation evidence, identify gaps, document findings, and recommend specific remediation.

AC-2 Account ManagementPartially EffectiveUser inventory, HR termination tickets, group membership, access-review records.Offboarding is manual and quarterly access reviews are not consistently completed.Implement a documented joiner-mover-leaver workflow, defined termination SLA, quarterly access certification, and evidence retention.
AC-6 Least PrivilegePartially EffectiveLocal admin groups, privileged cloud roles, admin account list.Some users retain standing administrative rights for convenience.Separate privileged accounts, reduce standing admin access, require approval, and review privileged memberships regularly.
IA-2 Identification & AuthenticationNeeds ImprovementMFA policies, authentication configuration, sign-in records.MFA is not enforced consistently for all workforce users and applications.Enforce MFA across the workforce, prioritize phishing-resistant authentication, and use conditional-access rules for elevated-risk sign-ins.
AU-2 Event LoggingPartially EffectiveEndpoint, identity, SaaS, and firewall logging settings.Important authentication and administrative events are logged, but coverage is inconsistent across systems.Define required event categories and maintain a log-source inventory showing coverage, ownership, and retention.
AU-6 Audit Record Review, Analysis, and ReportingNeeds ImprovementAlert rules, review procedures, incident tickets.There is no documented daily/weekly review cadence or accountable owner for several high-value alerts.Assign alert owners, define triage SLAs, document escalation criteria, and measure closure quality.
CM-2 Baseline ConfigurationPartially EffectiveEndpoint configuration standards, deployment scripts, exception notes.A practical baseline exists but is not formally versioned and exceptions are not consistently approved.Create an approved version-controlled baseline with owners, review dates, and exception records.
RA-5 Vulnerability Monitoring and ScanningNeeds ImprovementScanner output, asset list, remediation tickets.Scan coverage is irregular and there is no consistently enforced remediation SLA.Define asset coverage, routine scanning, severity-based remediation timelines, exception handling, and vulnerability-aging metrics.
IR-4 Incident HandlingPartially EffectiveDraft incident plan, escalation list, prior incident notes.Roles are generally understood but the plan has not been formally exercised.Approve the plan, assign role backups, run tabletop exercises, and track corrective actions to closure.
CP-9 System BackupPartially EffectiveBackup job logs, schedules, storage configuration.Backups run regularly, but documented restore tests are inconsistent.Maintain a protected backup copy and perform scheduled restore tests with recorded results and recovery issues.
SI-2 Flaw RemediationNeeds ImprovementPatch reports, update policies, exception tickets.Critical patch aging varies by system and exceptions do not always include risk acceptance.Define severity-based patch SLAs, exception approval, overdue reporting, and verification after remediation.

Assessment Method

How I would validate effectiveness

E

Examine

Policies, procedures, configuration exports, tickets, inventories, logs, reports, screenshots, approvals, and prior review evidence.

I

Interview

Control owner, administrator, business owner, help desk, HR, or other stakeholders who operate or depend on the control.

T

Test

Sample accounts, validate settings, trace an offboarding case, confirm logging, review vulnerability aging, or perform a restore test.

F

Find

Document what was expected, what evidence showed, the risk created by the gap, the owner, remediation, and verification criteria.

Assessment conclusion

The sample environment has basic controls in place, but several are not yet reliable enough to provide consistent assurance. The highest-priority gaps are workforce MFA, vulnerability/patch governance, monitoring ownership, and repeatable evidence that controls continue operating over time.
