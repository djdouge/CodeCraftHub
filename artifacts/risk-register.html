Risk Register & Treatment Plan

A simulated qualitative risk register for fictional HarborPoint Services. Likelihood and impact are scored from 1–5; inherent risk equals likelihood × impact. Target residual risk reflects the intended state after planned controls are implemented and verified.

Fictional Organization8 RisksQualitative 5×5 MethodOwner + Treatment + Residual Risk
Scenario: HarborPoint Services is a fictional 250-person hybrid professional-services company using Windows endpoints, cloud productivity tools, SaaS CRM, remote access, and customer PII. This is portfolio work, not a real client assessment.

Rating guide: 1–4 Low, 5–9 Moderate, 10–25 High. The purpose is consistent prioritization, not false mathematical precision.

Treatment Roadmap

90-day priority sequence

30

Days 0–30

R-01 credential controls and R-03 vulnerability/patch backlog. These combine high likelihood with high business impact.

60

Days 30–60

R-02 access lifecycle, R-04 centralized logging, and R-08 incident-response structure.

90

Days 60–90

R-05 tested recovery, R-06 vendor-risk process, and R-07 data-sharing restrictions.

✓

Verification

Close actions only after evidence shows the new control operates as intended; otherwise keep the residual risk open.

Evidence I would request

  • MFA and conditional-access configuration exports
  • Active account and privileged-group membership reports
  • Vulnerability scan results, patch reports, exception tickets, and aging metrics
  • Central logging source inventory, alert rules, and triage records
  • Backup job records and documented restore-test results
  • Vendor security reviews and contract security clauses
  • External-sharing/DLP configuration and exception records
  • Incident-response plan, tabletop record, and corrective actions
